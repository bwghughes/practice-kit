Privacy & Security

Your clients' data stays on your device. Full stop.

PracticeKit was built from the ground up for the most sensitive data a counsellor holds — clients' mental health and safeguarding records.

📵

No Cloud. Ever.

Data is stored exclusively on your iPad. Cloud sync is permanently disabled. Your client records never pass through any server — ours or anyone else's.

🔐

AES-256 Encrypted Backups

When you export, your data is encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a password you choose. The key is derived with 600,000 rounds of PBKDF2 — the same standard used by financial institutions.

👁️

Face ID & Auto-Lock

PracticeKit locks automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity and requires Face ID or Touch ID to unlock. The app hides its contents when you switch away — protecting sensitive data from view.

🧹

EXIF Data Stripped

Photos attached to session notes are stripped of all metadata — GPS location, device information, timestamps — before storage.

📊

Privacy-First Error Reporting

Anonymised crash data is sent to Sentry to help us fix bugs. No client names, notes, or safeguarding details are ever included. Personal data transmission is permanently disabled.

🗑️

True Deletion

When you delete a client record, all associated sessions, notes, photos, and safeguarding records are permanently removed from the device — no hidden copies.

Built by a counsellor, for counsellors

Helen knew there had to be a better way.

Helen Hughes has been a BACP-registered counsellor for over a decade. Across her practice in Staffordshire and Derbyshire — seeing clients in Burton on Trent, Lichfield, and beyond — she's helped hundreds of people through anxiety, trauma, depression, and the full complexity of human experience.

But behind every client session, there was a stack of admin that never got easier. CPD reflection in a spreadsheet. Supervision impact logged on paper. Session notes in one app, safeguarding records somewhere else. Professional records scattered across half a dozen places—none of them designed for how a counsellor actually works.

"I kept thinking: I have a Diploma in Counselling, a decade of practice, and I'm spending hours every month wrestling with spreadsheets just to prove I'm doing my job properly. There had to be a better way."

So Helen built one. PracticeKit started as a private place for the records surrounding her own work—structured enough to be useful, simple enough to keep current, and kept away from a vendor cloud database.

Who PracticeKit is for

Designed for qualified counsellors and therapists

PracticeKit is a practitioner-only professional-record app for qualified counsellors, psychotherapists and therapists practising in the UK. Clients do not book, pay, message or create accounts through it.

It is for practitioners who want more structure than paper or spreadsheets, without placing their working records in a practice-management cloud.

⚠️ PracticeKit is not a medical device and does not provide clinical advice, diagnosis, therapeutic content or automated safeguarding decisions. It supports record keeping; professional and legal responsibility remains with the practitioner.
