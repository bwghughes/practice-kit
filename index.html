Common questions

Before you register

Straight answers for counsellors evaluating PracticeKit ahead of launch.

What is PracticeKit?

A private professional-record app for UK counsellors and therapists. Client notes, reflective CPD, supervision and safeguarding in one iPad app—without a cloud database, client accounts or AI session transcription.

Where is my client data stored?

Working records stay on your iPad. PracticeKit does not synchronise client data to a PracticeKit cloud database. Optional backup exports are encrypted with a password only you know.

Does it record or transcribe sessions?

No. PracticeKit does not record, transcribe or generate notes from counselling sessions. You decide what belongs in the professional record.

Who is it for?

Qualified counsellors, psychotherapists and therapists practising in the UK. Clients do not book, pay, message or create accounts through it. It is not a medical device and does not provide clinical advice.
