Helen Hughes

BACP Registered Therapist · Diploma in Counselling · 10+ years in practice

Helen founded PracticeKit after experiencing first-hand how much time counsellors spend maintaining essential professional records across disconnected systems.

Visit Helen’s professional website ↗

Counselling experience behind the product

Helen has worked as a counsellor for more than a decade in Staffordshire and Derbyshire, supporting people through anxiety, trauma, depression and other complex experiences.

Her professional work involves the responsibilities familiar to practitioners throughout the counselling professions: maintaining appropriate client records, reflecting in supervision, keeping skills and knowledge current, documenting concerns and protecting confidentiality.

Why she created PracticeKit

PracticeKit began as a response to fragmented administration. CPD activities might be kept in one spreadsheet, supervision somewhere else, and client or safeguarding records in separate systems that were never designed to work together.

Helen’s aim was to create one clear, private place for these professional records while keeping the practitioner—not the software—in control of professional decisions.

“PracticeKit is the tool I always wished existed. Everything you need to keep core professional records organised—in one place, on your iPad, completely private.”

Role at PracticeKit

Helen brings the practitioner perspective to product priorities, terminology and workflows. PracticeKit’s design is informed by lived experience of counselling administration, while recognising that requirements vary by practitioner, membership body, client group, employer and jurisdiction.

PracticeKit therefore supports record keeping rather than presenting software prompts as professional, ethical or legal decisions.
